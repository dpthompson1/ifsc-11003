Vinyl Pressings

Vinyls became the sole way to listen to music at home. First introduced in the 1910s, the format was restricted to a handful of songs.

In the late 1940's, LP (albums) offered a chance for bands to release their music as full-length projects instead of individual recordings the last few years had to offer.
