The Radio

The radio was built on the discovery of electromagnetic waves by Heinrich Hertz in the 1880s. After nearly a century of research and development.

In the mid-1890s, Guglielmo Marconi invented the first long-distance device to send long-distancce radio waves.

Finally, on December 23rd, 1900, Reginald A. Fessenden sent the first to send audio by electromagnetic waves over a mile away.

In 1906, a little over six years later, Fessenden would be the first to make a public (wireless) broadcast.
